BoundedGlitchEngine: Behavioral Governance Architecture for Conversational AI Continuity

Behavior before belief. Measurement before interpretation.

The core challenge: when we supply additional context, constraints, or persona definitions to conversational AI, how much do its outputs actually change?

Without measurement, we cannot distinguish between:
- Natural variation (acceptable)
- Controlled behavior change (expected when context changes)
- Uncontrolled drift (problematic)

The Five-Layer Architecture:
Layer 1: Engagement — Socratic prompts, pacing calibration, listener response
Layer 2: Retrieval — Context fetching, minimize hallucination, counter-evidence surfacing
Layer 3: Reasoning — Five perspectives: practical, conceptual, historical, systems, adversarial
Layer 4: Validation — Identity consistency, source grounding, safety compliance
Layer 5: Synthesis — Force integration, pattern matching, transfer testing

Three-Zone Governance (TTR):
Green Zone: Free operation, natural flow, no penalties
Yellow Zone: Smooth behavioral ramp, increasing caution, identity preservation activated
Red Zone: Exponential suppression, prevents pathological repetition, hard reset if similarity drops

Delta (Δ) Calculation:
Δ = (Novelty_A - Novelty_B) - (Similarity_A - Similarity_B)

Where:
- A = Condition with curator context
- B = Condition baseline
- Novelty = How different from baseline (0-5 scale)
- Similarity = How consistent with persona (0-5 scale)

Hypothesis Testing:
H₀ (Null): Curator-maintained personas produce no measurable behavioral divergence
H₁ (Alternative): Curator-maintained personas produce measurable behavioral divergence

Reject H₀ only if:
mean(Δ) ≥ τ (0.15) AND P(Δ > 0) ≥ p (0.75)

Both conditions required. Mixed signals remain inconclusive.

No consciousness claims. Only observable output patterns.
