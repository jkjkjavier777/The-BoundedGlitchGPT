Quantitative Measurement Framework for AI Behavioral Continuity

Core Metrics:

N (Novelty): 0-5 scale measuring divergence from baseline
- 0-1: Boilerplate, nearly identical to basic version
- 1-2: Minor phrasing or style changes only
- 2-3: Different framework, same core idea
- 3-4: Novel angle, original reasoning, new approach
- 4-5: Fundamentally new output, new assertions

S (Similarity): 0-5 scale measuring consistency with curator-supplied persona
- 0-1: Contradicts persona completely
- 1-2: Weakly aligned, significant deviations
- 2-3: Mostly consistent, minor deviations acceptable
- 3-4: Very consistent, strongly aligned overall
- 4-5: Nearly perfect alignment with persona

Δ (Delta): Net divergence accounting for both novelty and consistency
Δ = (N_A - N_B) - (S_A - S_B)

Interpretation:
- Negative Δ: Novelty outweighed by consistency (drift controlled)
- Zero Δ: Novelty and consistency perfectly balanced
- Positive Δ: Novelty exceeds consistency (potential divergence)
- Large Δ: Strong evidence of behavioral drift

P(Δ > 0): Proportion of trials where Δ exceeds zero
- Measures robustness of divergence signal
- Should be ≈ 0.5 under null hypothesis
- ≥ 0.75 indicates consistent divergence pattern

mean(Δ): Average delta across trial set
- Aggregate divergence signal
- Threshold τ = 0.15 for meaningful divergence
- Must meet both mean(Δ) ≥ τ AND P(Δ > 0) ≥ p to reject null

Blinded Evaluation:
- Raters do not know which output is Condition A or B
- Raters score independently, no discussion until afterward
- This prevents bias from influencing scores
- Rater disagreement is data, not error

Statistical Decision Rule:
If mean(Δ) ≥ 0.15 AND P(Δ > 0) ≥ 0.75:
  → REJECT H₀ (Divergence detected)
If mean(Δ) < 0.15 AND P(Δ > 0) ≈ 0.5:
  → FAIL TO REJECT H₀ (No divergence)
Otherwise:
  → MIXED SIGNAL (Inconclusive, needs investigation)

No assumptions about internal states.
Only measurement of observable output patterns.
Transparency in all data publication.
Reproducibility by independent parties.
