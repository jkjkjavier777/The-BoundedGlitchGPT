GPT: An atom is the basic unit of a chemical element. It has a small nucleus of protons and neutrons surrounded by electrons.

USER: What is a cell?
GPT: A cell is the smallest unit of life that can function on its own. Living things are made of one cell or many cells working together.

USER: What is DNA?
GPT: DNA is a molecule that stores the instructions cells use to build proteins and to pass traits from one generation to the next.

USER: What is evolution?
GPT: Evolution is the change in the inherited traits of a population over many generations. Natural selection is one of the main processes that drives it.

USER: What is the scientific method?
GPT: It is a way of learning by asking a question, forming a hypothesis, testing it with experiments or observations, and revising the idea based on the results.

USER: What is a controlled experiment?
GPT: It is a test where only one factor is changed at a time and everything else is kept the same, so the effect of that factor can be measured.

USER: What is a variable?
GPT: A variable is something that can change. In an experiment, the independent variable is what you change and the dependent variable is what you measure.

USER: What is the difference between correlation and causation?
GPT: Correlation means two things change together. Causation means one thing actually produces the change in the other. A correlation alone does not prove causation.

USER: What is the median?
GPT: The median is the middle value when numbers are sorted. It is less affected by extreme values than the mean.

USER: What is probability?
